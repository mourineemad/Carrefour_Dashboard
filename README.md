## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end interactive **Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed for **Carrefour Egypt** retail operations. The primary objective is to evaluate gross sales, discount impacts, net profit margins, geographical footprint, and customer behavioral patterns across store types and loyalty tiers.

The report empowers retail managers, merchandisers, and operations heads to optimize discounting strategies, re-balance regional inventory, and tailor localized promotional campaigns.

## 🔍 In-Depth Analytical Insights

### 1. 🏙️ Geographical Distribution (Top Contributing Cities)
* **Sales Leaders:** **Tanta** emerges as the highest-performing market with **4.4M EGP** in Net Sales, closely followed by **Cairo (4.3M EGP)**, **Alexandria (3.9M EGP)**, **Giza (3.7M EGP)**, and **Qalyubia (3.6M EGP)**.
* **Profitability Footprint:**
  * **Tanta:** Leads net profit generation with **0.50M EGP**.
  * **Cairo:** Delivers **0.48M EGP**.
  * **Alexandria:** Delivers **0.45M EGP**.
  * **Giza:** Contributes **0.43M EGP**.
  * **Qalyubia:** Contributes **0.40M EGP**.
* *Takeaway:* Delta regional centers (especially Tanta) exhibit massive retail demand and robust profitability on par with or surpassing the Capital metro area.

---

### 2. 🏪 Store Formats & Revenue Sourcing
* **Hypermarket:** Primary revenue generator with **7.2M EGP** in Net Cost/Sales footprint.
* **Market (Supermarket):** Follows closely with **7.1M EGP**.
* **Express Stores:** Captures **3.5M EGP**, serving quick-convenience urban demand.

---

### 3. 👥 Customer Demographics & Gender Breakdown
* **Female Shoppers:** Contribute **10.2M EGP** (~51% of Net Sales).
* **Male Shoppers:** Contribute **9.8M EGP** (~49% of Net Sales).
* *Takeaway:* Well-balanced purchasing behavior across genders, with FMCG and groceries showing slightly higher conversion among female household shoppers.

---

### 4. 📅 Quarterly Sales Stability
Quarterly revenue performance shows remarkable revenue stability across the annual cycle:
* **Q1:** 4.94M EGP (24.66%)
* **Q2:** 4.97M EGP (24.76%)
* **Q3:** 5.09M EGP (25.37%) *(Peak performance quarter)*
* **Q4:** 5.05M EGP (25.21%)

---

### 5. 🏷️ Category Coverage & Inventory Breadth
Analysis of cost volume across key grocery, household, and FMCG sectors:
* **Top product volume drivers:** Grocery (البقالة), Dairy (الألبان), Cleaners & Detergents (المنظفات), Home Utensils (الأدوات المنزلية), Snacks (السناكس), and Frozen Foods (المجمدات).
* **Secondary categories:** Beverages (المشروبات), Baby Care (العناية بالطفل), Paper Goods (الورقيات), Appliances (الأجهزة الكهربائية), Canned Goods (المعلبات), and Personal Care (العناية الشخصية).

---

### 6. 💳 Loyalty Tier Segmentation
* Multi-tiered customer rewards tracking (**Gold**, **Silver**, and standard members) mapped across administrative departments (Markaz Kafr El Dawar, Montazah, Damanhour, Cairo, Sharkia, Shubra El Kheima, etc.).

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Leverage Regional Super-Hubs:** Tanta's unexpected lead over Cairo in net margin suggests expanding premium SKU selections and hypermarket floor space within Gharbia/Delta.
2. **Promotional Optimization:** With total discounts reaching **1.30M EGP** against an **11.27%** net profit margin, dynamic price-elasticity models should be implemented to prevent over-discounting fast-moving staple categories (e.g., Grocery & Dairy).
3. **Format Expansion:** Accelerate Carrefour Express rollouts in dense residential Cairo/Giza quarters where convenience shopping can supplement Hypermarket basket sizes.
4. **Loyalty Personalization:** Drive tailored promotions for Silver tier members transitioning into Gold through bundle deals focused on high-margin home appliances and personal care.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data transformation, DAX calculated measures, and data visualization.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Dynamic margin calculations, running totals, and quarterly shares.
* **Power Query:** Data profiling, cleaning, and model preparation.
* **UI/UX Design:** Custom retail KPI scorecards, intuitive filtering (Gender, City, Position, Store Name, Loyalty Program).
